# Research Interests

My research interests lie at the intersection of communication studies, computational methods, and emerging digital technologies. I am interested in understanding communication phenomena through quantitative approaches, computational modeling, and interdisciplinary perspectives.


---

## Measurement & Causal Inference

I explore quantitative approaches in communication research, focusing on measurement, research design, and causal identification.

My interests include how communication effects can be measured, how research questions can be transformed into empirical designs, and how quantitative methods contribute to understanding communication processes.


---

## LLM-based Cognitive and Behavioral Modeling

I am interested in exploring how large language models (LLMs) can contribute to understanding cognition, behavior, and communication processes.

This includes investigating the interaction between artificial intelligence, human cognition, and digital communication environments.


---

## Computational & Intelligent Communication

I study communication phenomena through computational approaches and emerging intelligent technologies.

My interests include social media analysis, digital communication, and data-driven approaches to understanding information production and dissemination.
