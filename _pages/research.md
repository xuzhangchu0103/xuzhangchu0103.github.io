My research lies at the intersection of communication studies, computational methods, and emerging digital technologies.

## Research Areas


### Measurement & Causal Inference


I explore quantitative approaches in communication research, focusing on measurement, research design, and causal identification.


My interests include developing empirical strategies to examine communication effects and understanding how methodological innovations contribute to communication research.


---

### LLM-based Cognitive and Behavioral Modeling


I study how large language models (LLMs) can contribute to understanding human cognition, behavior, and communication processes.


My interests include the interaction between artificial intelligence, human cognition, and digital communication environments.


---

### Computational & Intelligent Communication


I investigate communication phenomena through computational approaches and emerging intelligent technologies.


My research interests include social media analysis, digital communication, and data-driven approaches to understanding information production and dissemination.
